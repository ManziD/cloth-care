Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mobile service.

Our team brings high-efficiency professional steam equipment that plugs directly into standard household outlets. They consume minimal wattage per garment, ensuring safe, rapid pressing without overloading your home electrical circuits.
Standard pickup and delivery is completed within 24 to 36 hours. We also offer same-day express service for orders placed before 9:00 AM upon request.
Every item undergoes fabric inspection prior to treatment. Delicate materials (silk, wool, linen, embellished gowns) are hand-steamed or cleaned with specialized gentle formulations under our 100% Garment Care Guarantee.
We accept Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel), cash upon delivery or completion, and online card payments. Invoices are provided after service completion.
