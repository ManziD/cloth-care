Suit and formal wear care in Kampala

Suits, gowns and coats need more care than a normal wash. We shape, steam and return them ready to wear.

Book on WhatsApp Price calculator

What we do

  • Shape the jacket, including the lapels and collar
  • Steam silk blouses and evening wear gently
  • Handle ceremonial and traditional wear by hand
  • Pre-treat stains with eco-friendly enzymes

Every item is inspected before treatment. We check the fabric and the stitching first.

Price

UGX 20,000 per suit or formal piece. Pickup and delivery is included. You pay after inspection.

How it comes back

Your suit is returned in a breathable suit bag. This keeps it fresh and protects the shape.

Standard turnaround is 24 to 36 hours. If you have a deadline, ask for same-day express when you book. Orders must be placed before 9:00 AM.

Good to know

Tell us about any stains when you book, and what caused them. Oil, wine and ink need different treatment.

Bring up missing buttons or loose threads too. We would rather know before we start.
