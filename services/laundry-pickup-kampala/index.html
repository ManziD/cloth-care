Laundry pickup and delivery in Kampala

Message us, hand over your bag, and get it back clean and folded. Pickup and delivery costs nothing extra.

Book on WhatsApp Price calculator

How it works

  • Book on WhatsApp or through our site. Pick a time that suits you.
  • Our valet collects your clothes from your door.
  • We sort by colour and fabric, wash, dry and fold.
  • We bring it back to you, bagged and ready for the wardrobe.

Price

UGX 20,000 per bag. One bag is about 5 kg of everyday clothes, towels and bedsheets. Pickup and delivery is included.

What we use

We use hypo-allergenic detergents. This suits people with sensitive skin and families with young children.

Gym wear, casuals and linens are all fine. Delicate items go through our fabric inspection first.

Turnaround

Most orders come back within 24 to 36 hours.

For a faster return, ask for same-day express. Place the order before 9:00 AM.

Regular customers

If you need laundry done every week, tell us. We can agree a regular pickup day with you.

Questions people ask

Is laundry pickup free?

Yes. Doorstep pickup and delivery is included for all orders.

How big is a laundry bag?

About 5 kg of everyday clothes, towels and bedsheets.

How long does laundry take?

Usually 24 to 36 hours. Same-day express is available for orders placed before 9:00 AM.

Which areas do you cover?

Kampala, Entebbe, Naguru and Kololo.

Ready to book?

Open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. No upfront payment.
