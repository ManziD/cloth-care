In-home steam pressing in Kampala

A wardrobe specialist comes to your home with a portable steamer and presses your clothes there. Nothing leaves your house, so nothing gets creased in transit.

Book on WhatsApp Price calculator

How a session works

You book a 3-hour slot. Our specialist arrives with a professional steamer and dummy presses.

The equipment plugs into a standard household outlet. It uses little power and will not overload your circuits.

Pressed clothes go straight back on hangers in your wardrobe.

Price

UGX 120,000 per 3-hour session. No upfront payment. You pay after the work is done and inspected.

What we press

  • Shirts, trousers and everyday work clothes
  • Suits and jackets
  • Silk blouses and delicate fabrics
  • Dresses and evening wear

We check each fabric before we start. Delicate items are hand steamed.

When it makes sense

Choose a session when you have a big pile to get through, an event coming up, or a wardrobe you want reset.

If you only have a few items, pickup pressing at UGX 2,500 per item may cost less. Message us and we will tell you which is cheaper for your load.

Questions people ask

Do I need special equipment at home?

No. We bring everything. You only need a standard power outlet.

Is steam safe for silk and wool?

Yes. We inspect each garment first and steam delicate fabrics gently by hand.

How long does a session take?

A session is 3 hours with one specialist.

Where do you work?

We cover Kampala, Entebbe, Naguru and Kololo.

Ready to book?

Open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. No upfront payment.
