How payment works

You pay nothing upfront. We inspect your items first and you pay after.

We take MTN and Airtel Mobile Money, cash on delivery or on completion, and card online. You get an invoice when the job is done.

How long it takes

Standard pickup and delivery takes 24 to 36 hours.

We also do same-day express for orders placed before 9:00 AM. Ask for it when you book.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to press a shirt in Kampala?

Pressing a shirt or trouser with ClothCare Kla costs UGX 2,500 per item.

Do you charge for pickup and delivery?

No. Doorstep pickup and delivery is included in our prices.

What counts as one laundry bag?

One bag is about 5 kg of everyday clothes, towels and bedsheets. It costs UGX 20,000, washed, dried and folded.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. We accept MTN and Airtel Mobile Money, cash and card payments.

Ready to book?

Open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. No upfront payment.
